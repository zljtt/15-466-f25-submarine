# Submarine

**Game Play**

Our game is a multiplayer submarine-combat experience set in the deep sea. Each player controls a submarine with a simple objective: locate the hidden black box in the depths and bring it to the submit point—all while avoiding enemy detection and surviving underwater threats.

Every submarine is equipped with a sonar (radar) system that reveals nearby objects.

- Static objects appear as green dots.

- Dynamic objects gradually shift to red based on their speed.

Players can activate a Radar Superscan by pressing R. This powerful scan highlights other submarines, the flag, and the return location over a much larger radius. However, using it comes with a risk: it exposes your submarine to all other players, making you vulnerable.

To engage in combat, the submarine can launch torpedoes with Space to destroy opponents. Players may also toggle their spotlight on or off using L, choosing between visibility and stealth.


The game features three distinct submarine types, each with unique strengths and weaknesses:

- Explorer – Has a low collision damage and equipped with a powerful radar capable of fast detection, the scan result appears gradually. 

- Fighter – Higher HP and a default radar system.

- Seeker – Has access to a strong and undetectable radar superscan, but its regular radar has a smaller range.
